**METAR DECODER** 

METAR is the weather report that pilots get and is encoded in such a way that some training is necessary to learn to read it. This app will help you understand your local METAR report. Always double-check important info.

_Available Features:_

- METAR decoding
- Dark/Light mode switching
- Dynamic text sizing
